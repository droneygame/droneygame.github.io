Common questions

How do I play?

Hold anywhere on the screen to fly up and let go to glide down. Fly through rings for speed and battery, double-tap to barrel roll, and keep away from the clouds below. When your battery runs low, a glowing ⚡ charging tube appears ahead. Fly through it to recharge.

How do live races work?

Tap ⚡ Race live on the title screen. Choose Race friends to invite up to 3 Game Center friends (or players nearby), or Race anyone to be matched with other players. After a 3-2-1 countdown everyone flies the same course at the same time, with the same abilities. Whoever flies farthest wins. You need to be signed in to Game Center and online.

I can't find anyone with "Race anyone." Why?

Random matching only works when other players are searching at the same moment. If no one turns up, try again later, or use Race friends to invite someone you know.

How do ghost challenges work?

After a run, tap 📨 Challenge a friend to send a link by Messages or any app. When your friend taps it, Droney opens and they race a replay of your run (your "ghost") on the exact same course. They can send their run right back. If Droney isn't installed, the link opens a page on this site instead.

Will my progress carry over to my other devices?

Yes. When you're signed in to Game Center, your best distance, checkpoints, orbs, unlocked drones and achievements are saved to your account and loaded on any iPhone or iPad where you play with the same account. If you played on two devices, Droney keeps the best of both.

Why don't I see my Game Center scores?

Make sure you're signed in to Game Center in the Settings app. Scores are sent after you finish a run.

Does Droney have ads or in-app purchases?

No. Droney has no ads and no in-app purchases.

Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Droney is made by an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to information when you play Droney or visit droneygame.com. The short version: we (the developer) don't collect your personal information. Droney has no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we don't run any servers that receive your data.

Information we collect

None. We don't collect, store or sell any personal information.

Information saved on your device

Your game progress (such as best distance, checkpoints, orbs and unlocked drones) and settings are saved on your device so the game can remember them. Deleting the app deletes this copy.

Game Center (Apple)

Droney uses Apple's Game Center. Game Center is run by Apple, and Apple's privacy policy applies to it. If you're signed in:

  • Scores and achievements are sent to Game Center for leaderboards.
  • Saved progress (best distance, checkpoints, orbs, unlocked drones and achievements) is saved to your Game Center account using Apple's Game Center Saved Games, which stores it in your iCloud account so it can follow you to your other devices. It contains no personal information. We can't see or access it.
  • Live races: when you join a live race, Game Center connects you with the other players in that race. During the race, your Game Center nickname, which drone you're flying, and your drone's position and distance are shared with those players so they can see you race. This information is sent through Apple's Game Center, is only used for that race, and isn't stored by us. There is no chat.

Ghost challenge links

If you choose to send a challenge, the link contains your run: the course, your drone's flight path, your distance, which drone you flew and your Game Center nickname. It goes only to the people you send it to, using the app you pick (like Messages). If someone opens the link on droneygame.com, the page reads it right in their browser. The run data is never sent to or stored by us.

This website

droneygame.com is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) to run and secure its service, under GitHub's privacy statement. The site itself uses no cookies, analytics or tracking.

Crash reports and feedback

If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in your device settings, or send feedback through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports or the feedback you send with us. We use it only to fix problems and improve the game.

Children

Droney is suitable for all ages. We don't collect personal information from anyone, including children. Live races and friend invites use Apple's Game Center, which follows the Screen Time and Family Sharing settings a parent sets on the child's device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
